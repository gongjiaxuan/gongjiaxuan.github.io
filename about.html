About me

Jiaxuan Gong · HCI researcher & interaction designer

I trained in visual communication design before completing my M.F.A. in Design Science at Jiangnan University. My research interests developed through work on VR acceptance and the design of a mobile AR application for traditional craft.

I am now working on a systematic review of generative AI for accessibility. I am interested in how these systems work for people in practice: what they help with, what they ask users to check, and how users can shape the interaction.

Alongside research, I design interfaces and build physical installations and web applications. These projects let me work through interaction ideas in a form people can use.
