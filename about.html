About me

HCI researcher and interaction designer

I studied visual communication design at Nanjing University of the Arts, then completed my M.F.A. in Design Science at Jiangnan University in 2026.

My research includes a mixed-methods study of VR acceptance, a mobile AR prototype for traditional craft, and a systematic review of 240 papers on generative AI and accessibility.

I also build web tools and physical installations. I am interested in how people understand these systems, control them and get help when an interaction breaks down.
