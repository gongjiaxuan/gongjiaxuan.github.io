About me

HCI researcher and interaction designer

I studied visual communication design at Nanjing University of the Arts, then completed my M.F.A. in Design Science at Jiangnan University in 2026.

My current work is a systematic review of 240 papers on generative AI and accessibility. I examine how disabled people participate in this research and what evidence supports its accessibility claims.

My earlier research studied museum VR acceptance and a mobile AR prototype for traditional craft. Alongside these studies, I build web tools and physical installations.

I use surveys and interviews to study how people experience technology. My work also includes statistical modelling, qualitative analysis and systematic reviews.
