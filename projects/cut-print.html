CUT / PRINT

A browser-based poster editor for cutting colour fields and reshaping vessels.

Draw, reshape and print

I designed and built CUT / PRINT to make composing a poster feel hands-on. A cut changes the relationship between two colour fields. A vessel changes shape through a gesture. Ink, halftone and paper give those actions a material character.

The tool opens with a sample you can edit immediately. Shuffle offers another starting composition; Start blank gives you an empty sheet.

The sample is already editable. Draw a diagonal across the sheet to change the cut. Select Shape the vessel, choose a second profile and drag the preview; release to update the poster. Choose a different ink pair. Print It previews field, form and type in three passes. Return to the editor, open the preview again and export a PNG with a 3600-pixel long edge.

Three changes to the same editable sample.

Draw the boundary

Choose Draw a cut and drag across the sheet. A guide follows your hand. Releasing redraws the colour fields along that line, reversing the largest crossing form.

Interaction choiceThe hand sets the angle and position. Only a light snap near horizontal or vertical assists the gesture.

Watch this gesture

Shape a vessel with a gesture

Choose two of six profiles. Drag sideways in the preview to blend them, or vertically to tilt the form. The poster updates when you release.

Interaction choiceA shaded preview makes the changing shape legible before it becomes a two-ink silhouette.

Watch the vessel change

Change the ink, keep the composition

Choose a pair in Ink & Paper. Both fields change, while the cut, text and vessel stay in place. The docket reports the resulting ink and paper settings.

Interaction choiceColour and material edits stay local. They do not ask for a new layout.
