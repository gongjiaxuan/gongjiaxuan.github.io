CUT / PRINT

Two inks. One sheet. Your composition.

I built CUT / PRINT to make two-colour posters in the browser. It lets people compose with type, vessel forms, photographs and geometric elements, then adjust the inks and paper before exporting an image.

Open CUT / PRINT

Editing the composition

Text and objects remain individually editable on the canvas. People can move, resize or remove an element without rebuilding the rest of the poster, and regenerate the whole composition in one move when they want a different start.

Print settings

Ink coverage, halftone and registration settings change the print texture. The application renders and exports with the Canvas API; photograph processing happens locally. The examples below were exported from the application.
