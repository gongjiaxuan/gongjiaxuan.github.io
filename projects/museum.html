Website redesign concept

NUA Museum

Give the art room.
Give visitors a clear route.

A homepage concept for the Nanjing University of the Arts Museum. Generous space, restrained typography and changing artwork images create a quieter way to browse exhibitions, education and the collection.

Design approach
Large areas of white space, sans-serif typography and image-led interaction.
Deliverable
Adobe XD homepage prototype with a recorded interaction demonstration.
Read the page structureWatch the interactionsCompare image states

A homepage,
not a wall of links.

The artwork introduces the museum. Three content areas then provide distinct routes through the page.

  1. Current exhibitions

    Exhibition imagery and concise supporting information.

  2. Education

    A separate area for museum learning activities.

  3. Collection

    Overlapping artwork images invite further browsing.

A different pace
for each section

The layout changes its alignment and image scale as the reader moves down the page. Exhibitions, education and the collection share a typographic language without becoming identical cards.

The images are
part of the navigation

The recorded prototype shows image changes, hover responses and vertical browsing. These states are part of one homepage design, rather than separate fully implemented pages.

One structure.
Several image states.

The original exports preserve alternate artwork states within the same layout. Comparing them shows what changes with interaction and what remains consistent.

Design intent and next checks

The intent is a relaxed, image-led visit. Before implementation, the image interactions would need keyboard and touch equivalents, reduced-motion behaviour, and clear links that do not depend on hover alone.

Sources: the original design statement, Adobe XD project, homepage exports and interaction recording. The brief describes white space, sans-serif type and interactive images as the design choices. No museum commission, live deployment or usability study is claimed.
