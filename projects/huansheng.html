Rebirth & Illusion

Botanical forms in an artificial environment.

Rebirth & Illusion considers the relationship between plant life and constructed environments. Metal, plaster and screens form an installation in which botanical imagery appears within artificial materials.

Project scope
Visual and spatial practice
Methods & tools
Digital media · Installation · Visual design

The work

The visual composition contrasts organic forms with manufactured surfaces. The installation and moving-image documentation present this relationship through material, light and spatial arrangement.
