Collaborative virtual exhibition concept

Rebirth & Illusion

A virtual exhibition of plant forms and artificial materials.

Metallic branches, sculptural vessels and moving botanical images form a rendered exhibition space.

Project context
Team project by 龚珈萱, 顾小漩 and 丁子轩, documented in 2022.
Medium
Rendered virtual exhibition, spatial composition and animated imagery.
Explore the material languageWatch the spatial filmRead the space

Plant forms in metal and sculpture

Metallic branches, layered vessels and pale sculptures sit beside images of living plants.

The virtual exhibition film

The film moves through the proposed space, showing the animation and the positions of objects and screens.

Screens and translucent layers

Screens and translucent layers divide the space. Plant imagery appears on the screens and in nearby sculptural objects.

Towards a physical installation

A physical version would need material and projection tests, a visitor route and evaluation of how people interpret the work.

Team project by 龚珈萱, 顾小漩 and 丁子轩, documented in 2022. These are rendered views and animation from a virtual exhibition proposal, not a built installation.
