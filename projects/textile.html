TEXTILE2050

An industrial landscape shaped by physical input.

TEXTILE2050 combines a digital factory environment with physical interaction. Pressure and distance sensing feed a real-time visual system, connecting changes in the installation to visitors' actions.

Project scope
Physical computing and spatial visualisation
Methods & tools
Arduino · TouchDesigner · Blender
Watch the prototype

From sensing to visual response

Pressure and ultrasonic sensors provide inputs to the visual system. The project combines sensor prototyping, a modelled environment and real-time visual output; the images and video document the installation and its construction.
