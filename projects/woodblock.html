Tablet interface prototype

Woodblock Reimagined

Explore the stories and making of Zhuxianzhen woodblock prints.

Reading the Chinese interface

历史溯源 = history; 年画故事 = print stories; 制作流程 = making process; 趣味谚语 = folk sayings. The title, 朱仙镇木版年画, means “Zhuxianzhen woodblock New Year prints”.

A tablet prototype with four routes: history, print stories, the making process and folk sayings. Readers can browse illustrations, open details and follow a timeline.

Project scope
Information architecture, illustrated interface design and an Adobe XD prototype.
Format
Original tablet screens and a recorded interaction walkthrough.

Four ways to explore the prints

The four routes use different layouts: a timeline for history, an image index for stories, a sequence for making, and illustrated passages for folk sayings.

Browse a print, then open its story

Selecting a print opens its image and explanation in an overlay. The page behind it remains visible, so readers can return to where they were.

Planning the content

The first content map covers woodblock prints broadly. The later version narrows the subject to Zhuxianzhen and organises its stories and making process.

Compare the original information maps

English guide to the content map

The later map groups the content into history, print stories, production, visual characteristics and folk sayings. Its making sequence is: prepare the wood → draw the design → transfer it → carve the block → print → dry.

What to test next

The prototype connects images with short explanations. Usability testing would check whether readers can find a story, close its detail view and return to the overview.

Adobe XD interface prototype. Screens, information maps and the walkthrough come from the original project. It has not been deployed or evaluated as an educational application.
