REMEMBERY

Connecting everyday support around dementia care.

REMEMBERY is a mobile service concept for people living with Alzheimer's disease and those who support them. The interface brings together personal records, family contacts and support services.

Project scope
Design concept and interactive prototype
Methods & tools
Figma · Interface design · Service concept
Watch the prototype

Designing around a support network

The concept considers four groups: people living with Alzheimer's disease, family caregivers, doctors and social workers. Their different information needs informed the organisation of screens and service functions.

The interface

The prototype brings personal records, familiar contacts and support services into a shared visual system. The work includes screen design, navigation and service touchpoints.

Project status

The work is a design concept and interface prototype. Its usability and accessibility assumptions still need evaluation with people living with dementia and their support network; it has not been clinically evaluated.
