Generative AI for Accessibility

Evaluation, participation and design responsibilities.

This ongoing systematic review examines how HCI research evaluates generative AI for accessibility. I focus on the evidence supporting accessibility claims, the participation of disabled people, and the work involved in checking or correcting generated outputs.

Project scope
Project lead and first author, with Zhengyang Lu and Feng Wang
Methods & tools
Systematic review · Evidence synthesis · Qualitative coding

Research focus

I examine the tasks, participants, settings and measures used to evaluate GenAI accessibility systems, including whether studies assess generated outputs alone or how people use those outputs to complete a task.

My work

I lead the review and developed a coding framework covering disability context, generative AI modality, accessibility task, evaluation methods, study setting and the participation of disabled users. The search and screening work covered 7,184 unique records and 703 full texts.

Current status

The synthesis and first-author manuscript are in preparation. The working title is “Generative AI for Accessibility in HCI: A Systematic Review of Evaluation, Participation, and Design Responsibilities”. The final included corpus is still being refined.
