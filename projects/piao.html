Physical-computing prototype

FLUTTER

A heartbeat becomes
a drifting line.

A fingertip sensor and an Arduino-based prototype connect bodily input to a balloon moving through an Unreal Engine landscape. The work explores a poetic relationship between an internal rhythm and a visible trail.

Project scope
Pulse-sensing hardware, a real-time 3D scene and a projection demonstration.
Evidence
Original demonstration film, Blueprint view, hardware footage and installation photographs.
Watch the prototypeFollow the inputSee the physical setup

The body is the input.
The landscape is the response.

The original film introduces the scene, shows the hardware and Blueprint logic, and demonstrates the moving balloon and coloured trail. Its captions describe changes in balloon speed and direction in relation to heart rate.

A visible path through
an otherwise invisible signal

The available documentation connects three parts: sensing at the finger, processing in the prototype, and movement in the projected scene. The images below are original photographs and frames, not a reconstructed technical diagram.

The trail is an artistic response to the input. It should not be read as a diagnosis, an emotion detector or a clinically validated measure of stress.

Keep the signal tangible

Holding a small wired object beside a large projected scene makes the relationship between the two scales visible: an intimate bodily signal is translated into movement across a landscape.

Questions for a next iteration

How should the prototype show that it is receiving a signal? What should happen if fingertip contact is lost? These feedback and recovery states would matter before testing the experience with a wider group of participants.

Sources: the original FLUTTER film and four physical-demo photographs. Hardware calibration, mapping parameters and any effect on mood are not established by the available documentation. The page presents a prototype demonstration, not a wellbeing intervention.
