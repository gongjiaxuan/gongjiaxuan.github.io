# Portfolio accompaniment

Two previously silent edits have separate, original instrumental scores:

- `remembery-walkthrough-scored.mp4`: 33 seconds, soft keyboard tones and sustained chords, 72 BPM.
- `dunhuang-animation-scored.mp4`: 18 seconds, pentatonic plucked tones and a quiet sustained layer, 80 BPM.

The note sequences and sounds were composed and synthesized for this website. No existing songs, recordings or sample libraries were used. The music is portfolio accompaniment, not original prototype or exhibition audio. Both cues fade in and out; playback starts only when the visitor presses a video or chapter control.

The video streams are copied without re-encoding. The silent source edits remain alongside these versions. Other project videos retain their existing audio.
