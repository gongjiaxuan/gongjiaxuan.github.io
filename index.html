About me

All about me →

I am a design researcher working at the intersection of design, human–computer interaction, and accessibility. My M.F.A. work at Jiangnan University combines quantitative methods (SEM, machine learning, survey design) with qualitative approaches (thematic analysis, semi-structured interviews) to investigate how people interact with emerging technologies.

I am currently applying for PhD programs in Design, HCI, and Information Science, seeking a research environment where I can deepen my work at the intersection of design and technology.
