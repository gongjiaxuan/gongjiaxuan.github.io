About

Hi, I'm Jiaxuan. I came to HCI through the long way — started in visual communication design, picked up Python somewhere along the way, and somehow ended up building AR prototypes and running structural equation models on 388-person datasets.

These days I'm finishing my M.F.A. at Jiangnan University, where my research sits at the intersection of human-AI interaction, accessibility, and immersive media. I build things (AR apps with Unity, interactive installations with Arduino + TouchDesigner), then test them with real users (surveys, interviews, SEM). I like the full loop — from making something people can actually use, to understanding why it works or doesn't.

My thesis explored how AR can bring traditional craftsmanship to life in museum spaces — using the SOR framework, TAM, and flow theory to understand what drives visitor engagement. Before that, I led a PRISMA systematic review on GenAI accessibility, screening 3,570 papers and coding 136 of them. I've published in Heritage, SAGE Open, Applied Sciences, and several other venues.

Outside of research: I design typefaces for fun, have strong opinions about museum exhibition design, and take too many photos. TOEFL 106, GRE 333+4.0 — mentioning this because you're probably wondering.

☀️ Summer 2026: I'm looking for a research internship in HCI — especially projects around AI-mediated communication, accessibility, or AR/VR. If you're working on something that needs a design-trained researcher who can prototype and run user studies, I'd love to chat.
