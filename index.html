About me

I am a design researcher at the intersection of human–computer interaction, accessibility, and human-AI interaction. My M.F.A. work at Jiangnan University combines quantitative methods (SEM via SPSS/AMOS, survey design, ANN) with qualitative approaches (thematic analysis, semi-structured interviews, PRISMA systematic review) to investigate how people interact with emerging technologies.

I am currently seeking summer research opportunities in Human-AI Interaction, LLM-mediated communication, and accessible design — where I can contribute my mixed-methods training to collaborative, prototype-driven HCI research.
