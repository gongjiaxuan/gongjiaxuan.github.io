About

I came to HCI through the long way — started in visual communication design, picked up Python somewhere along the way, and somehow ended up building AR prototypes and running structural equation models on 388-person datasets. My M.F.A. at Jiangnan University sits at the intersection of human-AI interaction, accessibility, and immersive media.

I like the full loop: build something people can actually use (Unity AR apps, Arduino installations, Figma prototypes), then test it with real users (surveys, interviews, SEM), then figure out why it works or doesn't. My thesis explored how AR can bring traditional craftsmanship to life in museum spaces — using SOR, TAM, and flow theory to understand visitor engagement.

Before that, I led a PRISMA systematic review on GenAI accessibility (3,570 papers → 136 coded). I've published in Heritage, SAGE Open, Applied Sciences, and a few other venues. Outside of research: I design typefaces for fun, have strong opinions about museums, and take too many photos. TOEFL 106, GRE 333+4.0.

☀️ Summer 2026: I'm looking for a research internship in HCI — especially projects around AI-mediated communication, accessibility, or AR/VR. If your lab needs someone who can prototype and run user studies, let's talk.
