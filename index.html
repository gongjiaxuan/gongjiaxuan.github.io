About me

All about me →

I research how people interact with emerging technologies — building prototypes with Unity and Arduino, testing them with real users through surveys and interviews, and modeling what drives adoption with structural equation models. My work sits at the intersection of human-AI interaction, accessibility, and immersive media. I came to HCI from a visual communication design background, which means I care about how things look and whether they actually work. My M.F.A. thesis explored AR for cultural heritage; my other projects range from a PRISMA systematic review on GenAI accessibility to LLM-mediated consumer behavior. I'm currently looking for a summer 2026 research internship — get in touch if your lab could use a design-trained researcher who can prototype and run user studies.
